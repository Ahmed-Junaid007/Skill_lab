Gemini + Playwright Amazon Search

Describe the product you want. Gemini can turn your request into a focused search phrase, then Playwright opens Amazon India in Chromium and collects product listings.

Enter a product request and start the search.
How it works: Flask runs Playwright and Chromium on your computer. If GEMINI_API_KEY is configured in the server environment, Gemini refines your natural-language request first. Without the key, Playwright searches the exact text you entered.
